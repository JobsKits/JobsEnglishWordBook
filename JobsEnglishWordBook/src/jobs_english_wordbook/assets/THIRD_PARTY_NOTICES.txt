Jobs EnglishWordBook — data provenance

Vocabulary, translations, examples and phrases:
https://github.com/kajweb/dict
Upstream describes data as crawled from Youdao vocabulary books, including New Oriental books.
No clear redistribution license is supplied by that repository. These data are retained for local personal study; obtain permission or replace the corpus before public distribution.
Exact source archive URLs, SHA-256 hashes and record counts are in coverage.json.
Changes by Jobs: Unicode normalization, case-insensitive headword merging, semicolon sense splitting, exact example deduplication, cumulative school-level sets and custom IELTS learning sets. No generated example sentences are inserted.
